A Greeting Worth Sharing

“Yiasou” is how Greeks say hello, and it is also what they say when glasses clink together. It is a wish for good health and good company.

An estiatorio is a Greek restaurant, a place to sit down, slow down and enjoy a proper meal. Put them together and you have our invitation to you.
