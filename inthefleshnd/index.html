About

Your idea. Our ink.

In the Flesh Tattoos is a Dickinson, North Dakota tattoo shop built around one simple promise: if you can think it, we can ink it. A tattoo is personal, and it should feel that way from the first conversation to the final line.

Start with a conversation

Tell us what you have in mind. A sketch, a memory, a phrase or just a feeling. Every great tattoo starts there.

Made for you

We work with you on placement, size and detail so the finished piece fits you and your story.

Wear it proudly

Good tattoos last a lifetime. We'll take the time to make sure you love yours for years to come.
