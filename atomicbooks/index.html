Atomic Books is an independent bookstore in Baltimore, Maryland. The focus is indie comics and small press, so the shelves are full of work from independent artists, writers and publishers.

When you shop independent, your money stays local and helps keep that kind of work in print. And it's a great way to find your next favorite book.
