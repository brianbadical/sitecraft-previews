About Us

A family name in architectural stonework

As the name says, Charles J. Precourt & Son, Inc. is a family business. We focus on one thing: natural building stone, and helping people find the right material for the job.

Natural stone brings character, texture and durability that few materials can match. Whether you are planning a new build, a renovation or a landscape project, it pays to talk with a supplier who knows the material.
