Our Story

A neighborhood coffee shop that never closes.

Bennu Coffee is Austin's 24-hour coffee shop. Whether it's early morning or the middle of the night, you'll find the lights on and the espresso flowing.

We built Bennu as a welcoming space for the whole community: somewhere to settle in with a laptop, meet a friend, crack open a textbook or just slow down for a while.
