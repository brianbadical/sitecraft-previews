Our Story

A Brunswick table since 2013

Enoteca Athena opened in Brunswick in 2013 and has earned a name for high-quality, rustic Mediterranean food.

The cooking shows the American influence on Greek and Italian cuisines, keeping the warmth and character of those traditions alive. It's a place for good food, good company and an unhurried evening.
